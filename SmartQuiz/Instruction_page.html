Rank Seekers - JEE Main Instructions

General Instructions:

  1. Total duration of the test is 3 hours.
  2. The test consists of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics sections.
  3. Each question carries 4 marks. 1 mark will be deducted for each wrong answer.
  4. No negative marking for unattempted questions.
  5. Use of calculators, mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic device is strictly prohibited.
  6. Do not refresh, close, or switch tabs during the test. It may lead to termination.
  7. Once you start, the timer will not stop under any circumstances.
  8. Click “Next” to move to the next question and “Mark for Review” if you wish to revisit.
  9. You must submit your test before the timer ends, else it will be auto-submitted.
  10. Ensure a stable internet connection and power backup before starting the test.
  11. Maintain honesty — any unfair means will lead to disqualification.

Exam Pattern:

  • Physics – 25 Questions
  • Chemistry – 25 Questions
  • Mathematics – 25 Questions
